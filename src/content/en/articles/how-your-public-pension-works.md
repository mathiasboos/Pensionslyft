## Three parts in one

The public pension (allmän pension) consists of the income pension (inkomstpension), the premium pension (premiepension) and, for those who have had a low income or none, the guarantee pension (garantipension).

### Income pension
16 percent of your pensionable income is credited to your income pension account. The money is not invested on the stock market but is revalued with the income index (inkomstindex), that is, wage growth in Sweden.

### Premium pension
2.5 percent is invested in funds that you choose yourself. If you make no choice, the money goes to AP7 Såfa, which has historically been both a cheap and a strong alternative.

### Guarantee pension
A basic safety net for those who have had a low lifetime income. It is phased out against the income pension.

## What you can influence

Three things decide the size: how much you earn, how long you work and when you start taking out the pension. Taking it out one year later noticeably increases the lifelong payout.
