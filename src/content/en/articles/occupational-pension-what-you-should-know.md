## Why the occupational pension is decisive

For many people the occupational pension (tjänstepension) makes up 25–35 percent of the total pension. It is paid by the employer, but you make the choices yourself.

## Three choices that matter most

1. **Fund choice or traditional insurance.** A unit-linked insurance gives a higher expected return but bigger swings.
2. **The fee.** The difference between 0.2 and 1.0 percent in annual fees is enormous over time.
3. **Survivor protection (återbetalningsskydd).** It gives your survivors security but lowers your own pension.

## Changing jobs?

Always check whether the new employer has a collective agreement. If there is no occupational pension, you should compensate with your own saving, often 4.5 percent of your salary as a rule of thumb.
