## The calculation that changes everything

Assume 500,000 kronor in capital, 2,000 kronor in monthly saving, 35 years to go and a 7 percent return before fees.

- With a 0.2 percent fee: about 8.9 million kronor.
- With a 1.2 percent fee: about 7.0 million kronor.

The difference, almost two million kronor, is pure fee cost.

## Fees to look out for

| Type | What it is | Reasonable level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fund fee | Management fee in the fund | 0.2–0.4 % for an index fund |
| Capital fee | The insurance company's fee on the capital | 0–0.3 % |
| Fixed fee | Kronor per year | 0–200 kr |

## How to lower them

Move old pension insurances to a cheaper manager, choose index-like funds in the premium pension (premiepensionen) and gather your private savings in an investment savings account (ISK) with a low fee. Use our compound interest calculator (in Swedish) to see the effect on your own savings.
