The Swedish Pensions Agency (Pensionsmyndigheten) has calculated the income base amount (inkomstbasbelopp) for 2027 at **86,600 kronor**, an increase of 3.84 percent. The amount is to be set by the government.

## What it means

- The pension-earning ceiling for the public pension (intjänandetaket), also called the contribution ceiling (avgiftstaket), is therefore expected to rise to 58,239 kronor a month.
- The threshold for state income tax (brytpunkten för statlig inkomstskatt) is expected to be 56,485 kronor a month.

## The balance ratio

The balance ratio (balanstalet) has been calculated at 1.1472. It is above 1, which means the income pension system has a surplus and therefore does not need to be balanced. The balance ratio is also below the limit of 1.1500, so there is no surplus to distribute. In 2027 the income pension (inkomstpensionen) will therefore be indexed only according to the income index (inkomstindex).

The so-called "gas" (gasen), which was legislated this year and applies from 2027, kicks in if the balance ratio is above 1.15. The surplus is then to be distributed.

## About the income base amount

The income base amount is recalculated every year using the income index, which measures general wage developments in the country. The amount is used, among other things, to calculate the highest income that gives the right to a public pension. It also determines the amount you should have left after any salary exchange (löneväxling), so that you do not lose out on contributions to the public pension system and on other social insurance benefits.

The Pensions Agency calculates the income base amount using the National Institute of Economic Research's (Konjunkturinstitutet) forecast of income developments over the coming years. The government then sets the amount in November.
