Next year, pensioners with an income pension (inkomstpension) get more money in their wallets. The government has decided on a rise of 2.2 percent from the turn of the year. For a pensioner with an income pension of SEK 18,500, that means around SEK 400 more a month. At the same time, the gap between pensioners who have worked and those who have not is growing.

The government has set the income index (inkomstindex) for 2027 at 236.84, which corresponds to an increase of 3.8 percent. The income index is the basis for the annual uprating of, among other things, the income pension and the supplementary pension.

After the follow-up indexation (följsamhetsindexering), the income pension rises by 2.2 percent at the turn of the year. Someone who has an income pension of SEK 18,500 today gets about SEK 400 more a month before tax.

## A bigger gap between pensioners

The rise also means that the so-called "respect gap" (respektavståndet) grows. It is the difference between the pension of someone who has worked and earned pension and someone who has had little or no income from work.

The reason is that the income pension follows the development of incomes, while the guarantee pension (garantipension) mainly follows the development of prices. When the income pension is uprated by more than the guarantee pension, the gap between the pensions grows.

According to the government, this means that working pays off more in terms of pension.

## The income ceiling rises to SEK 58,200 a month

The government has also set the income base amount (inkomstbasbelopp) for 2027 at SEK 86,600. Among other things, it affects how much income from work forms the basis for accruing the public pension.

The highest income that gives pension rights is therefore SEK 58,200 a month in 2027.

## No extra pension payout in 2027

There will, however, be no extra pension payout, the so-called "gas" (the pension accelerator), next year.

The balance ratio (balanstal) for 2027 has been set at 1.1472. That means the pension system's assets exceed its liabilities by just under 15 percent.

Under the agreement that the Pension Group made in August 2025, the gas is to be activated only when assets exceed liabilities by more than 15 percent. The limit is therefore not reached for 2027.

Pensioners therefore get the ordinary rise in the income pension, but no extra boost through the gas.
