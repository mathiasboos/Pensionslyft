After intensive negotiations over the weekend, LO, the Confederation of Swedish Enterprise (Svenskt Näringsliv) and PTK have agreed new terms for pensions, insurance and transition support. LO's bargaining secretary (avtalssekreterare) describes the deal as a "big victory". Among the changes are a higher age limit for occupational pension (tjänstepension), from 65 to 67, and improved insurance terms. But one of LO's most important demands is still missing.

It was late on Sunday that the parties announced that they had reached an agreement after the weekend's final negotiations. The deal covers around two million employees and brings several changes to the pensions and insurances under collective agreements.

LO's bargaining secretary Veli-Pekka Säikkälä describes the result as a "big victory". In an interview with the newspaper Arbetet he highlights in particular that the age limit for accruing occupational pension is raised from 65 to 67. The same increase applies to transition support and severance pay in case of unemployment.

But the changes will not all be introduced at the same time. The new age limit for occupational pension within Avtalspension SAF-LO applies only from 1 January 2028. Several other improvements take effect already in 2027.

## The demand for occupational pension from the first day of work remains

One issue where LO did not get its way is the demand that employees start accruing occupational pension already from the first day of work. Today, accrual to Avtalspension SAF-LO starts only at age 22.

LO's press office confirms to Pensioner & Förmåner that the demand stands and will be raised in coming negotiations.

– It is always so in negotiations that you do not get everything through, there are always compromises, says LO's press secretary Jens Lundberg to Pensioner & Förmåner.

## How the pensions and insurances improve

The agreement contains several changes for employees covered by the insurances under collective agreements.

**Avtalspension SAF-LO**

- The age limit for pension contributions is raised from 65 to 67 from January 2028.
- The lower age limit for accrual stays at 22.

**Transition support and severance pay**

- The upper age limit is raised from 65 to 67.
- The lower age limit of 40 is removed.
- It becomes easier to qualify for severance pay. Someone who has been employed for five years and is dismissed due to redundancy can get around SEK 50,000.

**Work-injury insurance, TFA**

- The deductible of SEK 600 is removed.
- The 30-day time limit for compensation for pain and suffering is abolished.

**Sickness insurance, AGS**

- The compensation, which has been unchanged since 2009, is tied to 7.5 price base amounts (prisbasbelopp) and is to be uprated automatically every year.

**Group life insurance, TGL**

- The age limit is raised from 65 to 67.

**Parental-leave supplement, FPT**

- The possibility of compensation is extended from one to three parental-leave periods per child.

## New negotiations ahead

The parties also agree to set up joint inquiries on, among other things, pensions, insurance and transition support. The work is expected to be finished in summer 2028 and will then form the basis for new negotiations.

The agreement must now be approved by the unions and employer organisations concerned. If all parties back the deal, the threat of the strike that LO had earlier given notice of for December is also removed.
