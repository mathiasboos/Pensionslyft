Many of those who end up with tax to pay when the tax return is settled (kvarskatt) are new pensioners. You can avoid it by asking for more tax to be withheld every month, or by applying for a tax adjustment (jämkning). The deadline to apply for a tax adjustment for the whole of 2027 is **8 November 2026**.

## Why many pensioners end up with tax to pay

Pensioners often receive pension from several different payers. Some also keep working, which makes it harder to work out the tax.

– The occupational pension companies usually withhold the same percentage in tax for everyone. That can mean that too little tax is withheld overall, which in turn leads to tax to pay, says Agneta Claesson, pension specialist at the Swedish Pensions Agency.

## Two ways to avoid tax to pay

- **Ask for extra tax on your pension.** If you are worried about ending up with tax to pay, you can ask the Pensions Agency to withhold extra tax on your pension payments. This is easy to do by logging in at pensionsmyndigheten.se.
- **Apply for a tax adjustment from the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket).** Both those who expect a lot of tax to pay and those who expect a large tax refund can ask for a tax adjustment. The right tax is then withheld from the start.

## What is a tax adjustment?

A tax adjustment (jämkning) means that the Tax Agency changes the preliminary tax, A-skatt, that is withheld from your pension every month. The aim is for the tax withheld to be as close as possible to the tax you will actually pay.

## What applies

- From 1 October you can apply for a tax adjustment for the following year.
- If you want the decision to apply from January 2027, the application must be received by 8 November 2026.
- You can also apply later, but the decision will then not cover the whole year. For the current year you can apply until 30 November.

## How to apply

You can apply in the Tax Agency's e-service if you have an electronic ID, for example mobile BankID. Otherwise you can use form SKV 4302, Jämkning (ändring av preliminär A-skatt), that is, tax adjustment (change of preliminary A-tax).

## When you have received the decision

- **Pension from the Pensions Agency:** Log in to the Pensions Agency's e-service Ändra skatteavdraget på din pension (change the tax deduction on your pension) and tick the box saying the tax is to be withheld according to your tax adjustment decision. You then do not need to send in the decision.
- **Several payers:** The decision shows, as a percentage, how much tax your main payer is to withhold. You give the decision to the main payer yourself. Other payers withhold 30 percent.

## Check the date

The deadline changes from year to year. For 2026 it was, for example, 9 November. Always read the current date on the Tax Agency's website before you apply.
