The Swedish Pensions Agency's (Pensionsmyndigheten) traditional insurance (traditionell försäkring) returned 9.66 percent in the period January–September 2026. That is a sharp improvement on the same period last year, when the return was 0.88 percent. It is mainly the strong development of the stock market that has contributed to the rise.

The total portfolio of the traditional insurance rose 9.66 percent in the first nine months of the year. Global equities were the strongest, with a return of 21.63 percent. Interest-bearing assets, on the other hand, fell 0.99 percent, while unlisted property shares dropped 2.50 percent.

At the end of September the managed capital was SEK 105.4 billion. Of this, SEK 55.3 billion was invested in global equities, SEK 47.8 billion in interest-bearing assets and SEK 2.3 billion in property.

The average return allocated to savers was 9.6 percent in the period, compared with 1.0 percent in the same period of 2025.

## Fossil-free equity portfolio

The Pensions Agency's equity portfolio is fossil-free. In the first nine months of the year it developed 0.84 percentage points weaker than a global equity index that also includes fossil companies. Since the portfolio became fossil-free in November 2019, its total development has been 1.70 percentage points lower than the comparison index.

## Over 400,000 pensioners have traditional insurance

When a saver retires, they can choose to place the premium pension capital in traditional insurance instead of keeping the unit-linked insurance (fondförsäkring). The Pensions Agency then takes over the management of the capital.

The insurance means that the pensioner gets a guaranteed monthly amount for life. On top of that, a supplement (tilläggsbelopp) can be paid. It is not guaranteed and can both increase and decrease depending on how the capital develops.

On 30 September 2026, about 402,000 pensioners had their premium pension in traditional insurance.
