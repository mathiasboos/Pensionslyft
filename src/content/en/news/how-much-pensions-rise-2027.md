The income pension (inkomstpensionen) rises by **2.2 percent** in January 2027, while the guarantee pension (garantipensionen) increases by **0.7 percent**. How large the increase is for you depends on which parts of the pension you have. The exact amounts will be available in January 2027.

## The price base amount and the income base amount in 2027

| Amount | 2026 | 2027 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price base amount (prisbasbelopp) | 59,200 kr | 59,600 kr | +0.7 % |
| Income base amount (inkomstbasbelopp) | 83,400 kr | 86,600 kr | +3.8 % |

## How pensions rise for different groups

The public pension often consists of several parts, including the income pension and the guarantee pension. Because many people have a combination of them, the total increase varies.

- A little over 1.2 million pensioners have income pension and/or supplementary pension (tilläggspension) without a basic safety net such as guarantee pension or housing supplement. Their income-based pension parts rise by 2.2 percent.
- Nearly 800,000 pensioners have both income pension and guarantee pension. For them the public pension rises by between 0.7 and 2.2 percent.
- Nearly 300,000 pensioners have a low income pension or none, and receive a large part of their pension from guarantee pension and housing supplement. The guarantee pension rises by 0.7 percent.
- About 7.2 million pension savers will see the balance on their income pension accounts revalued by 3.8 percent.

## Why pensions rise by different amounts

The income pension follows income developments in society. For 2027 the balance on the pension accounts is revalued by 3.8 percent, while the payments to pensioners increase by 2.2 percent.

The difference is because the income pension is calculated with an advance of 1.6 percent. The advance makes the pension higher at the start of the payout period. After that an annual adjustment is made, so-called adjustment indexing (följsamhetsindexering), where the advance is deducted from the income development.

The guarantee pension instead follows price developments through the price base amount. It rises from 59,200 kronor in 2026 to 59,600 kronor in 2027, an increase of about 0.7 percent.

According to the forecast, the income base amount rises from 83,400 kronor in 2026 to 86,600 kronor in 2027, an increase of about 3.8 percent. The final amount is set by the government in November.

## When will you find out how much your pension rises?

The Swedish Pensions Agency (Pensionsmyndigheten) can give the exact amounts only in January 2027. Pension notices are then sent to pensioners, and the information is also available on My pages (Mina sidor) at [pensionsmyndigheten.se](https://www.pensionsmyndigheten.se/).

The development of the premium pension (premiepensionen) is calculated at the end of December and also affects the total pension for those who have a premium pension.
