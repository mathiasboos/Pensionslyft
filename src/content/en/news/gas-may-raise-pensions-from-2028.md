After several years without any payout, the new pension "gas" (gasen, the pension accelerator) may start to take effect in 2028. According to the Swedish Pensions Agency's forecast, both today's pensioners and those who are still earning pension rights will share in the surplus in the pension system.

The Pensions Agency assesses that the gas will boost pensions for three years in a row: 2028, 2029 and 2030. The combined boost is estimated at 1.22 percent over the period.

For a person with an average income pension (inkomstpension), that means about SEK 170 extra, counted as an accumulated effect over the three years.

– Our forecast shows that the so-called gas boosts earned pension for the first time in 2028 and keeps doing so in 2029 and 2030, says Stefan Granbom, analyst at the Swedish Pensions Agency.

The announcement comes after the agency earlier concluded that the surplus in the pension system is not large enough for any payout in 2027. At the turn of the year, the income pension is instead expected to be uprated by 2.2 percent.

## The gas is to distribute the surplus

In June 2026 the Riksdag decided to introduce the so-called gas from 2027. The aim is to let surpluses in the pension system benefit both today's pensioners and future pensioners.

The payout is to strengthen both income and supplementary pensions (tilläggspension) already being paid out and the pension rights (pensionsrätter) of those who are still working.

The forecast is based on macroeconomic assumptions from the National Institute of Economic Research (Konjunkturinstitutet). The outcome may therefore change if economic developments differ from what was assumed.
